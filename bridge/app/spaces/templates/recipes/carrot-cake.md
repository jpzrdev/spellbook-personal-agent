# Carrot cake

A soft cake with a chocolate topping.

## Ingredients
- 3 medium carrots
- 3 eggs
- 1 cup of oil
- 2 cups of flour
- 1½ cup of sugar
- 1 tablespoon of baking powder

## Steps
1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C. (10 min)
2. Blend the carrots, eggs and oil.
3. Mix with the flour and sugar, then fold in the baking powder.
4. Bake until a toothpick comes out clean. (40 min)

## Notes
Better with a bit less sugar.
