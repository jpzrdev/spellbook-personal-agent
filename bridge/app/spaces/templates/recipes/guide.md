**Recipes** keeps the recipes you cook. Each recipe is a note in `spaces/recipes/`.

- **Cards:** your recipes with a photo, time, servings and rating. Filter by kind, tag or whether you tried it.
- **Details:** time, servings, kind, rating, tried and tags, editable in place. A photo is a web address or a file in the memory.
- **Ingredients:** a checklist to use while cooking. The checks live only on the screen (the recipe isn't changed).
- **Steps:** the method as steps. A time in parentheses, like "Bake (40 min)", gets a timer. *Focus mode* shows one step at a time.
- **Notes:** free text.

**Buttons**
- **Shopping list:** turns the ingredients into tasks tagged #shopping (no AI; repeated ones are skipped).
- **Adapt servings:** the assistant writes the ingredients for another number of servings in an **Adapted** section (the original stays). It runs in the background and the page updates by itself.

**By chat:** "save this recipe: …" (pasted text or a link) adds a recipe; "I made the carrot cake, 5 stars" updates it.
