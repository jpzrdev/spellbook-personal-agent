**Workouts** keeps your workout plans. Each workout (e.g. "Upper A") is a note in `spaces/workouts/`.

- **List:** your workouts with their focus, duration and when you last did each one. Filter by focus.
- **Details:** focus, duration and last done, editable in place.
- **Exercises:** the plan as steps. A step with a time in parentheses, like "Rest (2 min)", gets a timer. *Focus mode* shows one step at a time, big, for the gym.
- **Progress:** a chart of the load of one exercise over time, from the log. Pick the exercise above the chart; *Table* shows the numbers.
- **Next session:** what to do next time (written by the assistant).
- **Notes:** free text.

**Buttons**
- **Log session:** a form that adds a row to the log (date, exercise, sets, reps, load) and sets "Last done". No AI; *Save and add another* keeps the date for the next exercise.
- **Suggest next loads:** the assistant reads the log and writes the next session's loads in **Next session**. It runs in the background (a few seconds to a minute) and the section updates by itself.

**By chat:** "log today's Upper A: bench 4×8 at 45 kg, overhead 3×10 at 22.5" adds the rows; "create a lower body workout with squats, lunges and calf raises" adds a workout.
