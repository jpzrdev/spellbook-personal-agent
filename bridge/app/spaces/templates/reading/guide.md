**Reading** keeps the books you want to read, are reading and have read. Each book is a note in `spaces/reading/`.

- **Board:** three columns (Want to read, Reading, Read). Drag a book to another column to change its status. Filter by genre.
- **Details:** author, status, progress (%), rating, the day you finished and genres, editable in place. A cover is a web address or a file in the memory.
- **Summary:** a short summary of your notes (written by the assistant).
- **Highlights** and **Notes:** your quotes and thoughts, as free text.

**Buttons**
- **Summarize my notes:** the assistant writes a 5-line summary of your highlights and notes in **Summary**. It runs in the background and the page updates by itself.

**By chat:** "add Dune by Frank Herbert to my reading list", "I'm at 40% of Deep Work", "finished Dune, 4 stars", "save this quote from Deep Work: …".
